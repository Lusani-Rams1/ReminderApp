Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common CampusSync questions.

What is CampusSync?

CampusSync is an academic management platform that helps students organise tasks, schedules, deadlines and academic responsibilities.

Do I need an account to use CampusSync?

An account is required to access personalised academic information and manage your own tasks and schedule.

Can I use CampusSync on my phone?

Yes. CampusSync is being designed to support both a web application and an Android application.

Will my information be available on both platforms?

The web and Android applications are designed to use the same backend and database so that your academic information can be synchronised.

Can I track assignment deadlines?

Yes. CampusSync allows students to organise tasks, identify high-priority work and monitor upcoming deadlines.

How do task rewards work?

CampusSync awards 10 points for every completed task, allowing students to track their progress and build consistency.

How do I get help?

Visit the Help Center or Contact Us page if you need additional assistance.
