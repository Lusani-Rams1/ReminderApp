Privacy Policy

How CampusSync handles information within the application.

1. Introduction

CampusSync is designed to help students manage academic tasks, schedules, deadlines and progress. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information the application may collect and how that information is used.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, CampusSync may store information such as:

  • Full name
  • Student number
  • Email address
  • Academic tasks
  • Task deadlines and priorities
  • Schedule information
  • Selected subjects

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide personalised academic management features, including task tracking, scheduling, reminders and progress monitoring.

4. Account Security

Users are expected to keep their account credentials confidential. The production version of CampusSync should use secure authentication and password hashing through the backend API.

5. Data Sharing

Personal academic information should not be publicly displayed or shared with other users without appropriate authorisation.

6. Data Storage

CampusSync is designed around a central backend and database so that authorised users can access their information through supported platforms.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as CampusSync develops and additional features are introduced.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the CampusSync team through the Contact Us page.
